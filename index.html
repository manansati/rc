How It Works

A simple, mindful process designed to help you find the right support, work through what matters, and move forward with greater clarity.

01 Reach Out

Start The Conversation

Take the first step by getting in touch. Tell us a little about yourself and what has brought you here, so we can understand how to begin.

02 Share

Talk About What You're Facing

During our initial conversation, share what you're experiencing, what feels difficult, and what you would like to work on. There is no need to have everything figured out before we begin.

03 Your Session

Work Together On What Matters

Meet with Rakesh through a personalised Zoom session where you can explore your concerns, gain a fresh perspective, and receive guidance based on your individual situation.

04 Keep Going

Continue With The Support You Need

If you feel ready to continue, further sessions can help you build on the progress you've made, explore deeper concerns, and keep working toward the changes you want in your life.
